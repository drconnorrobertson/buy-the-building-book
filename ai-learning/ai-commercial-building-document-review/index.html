Prepare a Commercial Building Document Review With AI

Organize authorized property records and specialist questions before a building purchase.

Original website companion resource. Published October 9, 2026. This guide is not an excerpt or a claim about the original book chapters.

Make a document inventory first

Commercial building decisions involve leases, inspections, title information, operating expenses, improvements, insurance and financing. Start by recording which documents you have, their dates and the property they cover. AI can help build that inventory and identify missing categories. It should not infer that an absent report is unnecessary or that an old report describes the current condition.

Create a question register

For each document, record the factual finding, source location, question, responsible reviewer and deadline. A roof inspection may establish remaining life and recommend further work. A lease may assign some maintenance responsibilities to occupants. These records need coordination. Ask AI to group related questions, while qualified reviewers interpret the actual technical and legal implications.

Keep roles explicit

An inspector evaluates physical conditions within the inspection scope. A title professional and attorney handle title and document issues within their roles. A lender confirms financing conditions. Your operating team evaluates business use. AI helps prepare summaries across those workstreams. It cannot provide a survey, inspect a structure or verify a lien simply by reading a marketing brochure.

Use an extraction prompt

Ask: From these supplied property documents, build a review register with document name, date, relevant section, factual finding, open question, responsible specialist and due date. Distinguish quoted document language from your summary. Do not conclude that the property is suitable or compliant. Identify which findings affect business occupancy, cash requirements or the purchase timeline.

Turn findings into the purchase plan

Reconcile required work with the operating schedule and available cash. Confirm which items must be resolved before closing and which become a funded ownership project. AI can prepare a draft milestone calendar after the team approves those decisions. The completed review should be understandable to another person: source documents, specialist conclusions, authorized actions and the evidence needed to mark each action complete.
